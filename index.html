Mazad Oman

Online Car Auction Platform for Oman

Car Listing & Image Upload

Users can easily upload car images and detailed information for sale. The platform is specifically designed for the Omani market, allowing sellers to showcase their vehicles with comprehensive details and multiple photos to attract potential buyers.

Email & Account Verification

Secure account verification system that sends verification codes to users' email addresses. This ensures that all accounts are verified and authentic, protecting both buyers and sellers from fraudulent activity and maintaining platform integrity.

Admin Panel with Approval System

The system includes a powerful admin panel that automatically sends email notifications when a new car is submitted for review. Admins can approve or reject listings, and users receive automatic notifications upon approval, ensuring transparency and trust.

Built-in Chat System

The platform features an integrated chat system that enables direct communication between buyers and sellers. This enhances trust and interaction, allowing users to negotiate, ask questions, and finalize deals efficiently within the platform.

CafeConnect

Mobile App for Café Discovery with Reward System

Location-Based Café Discovery

CafeConnect helps users find the nearest cafés based on their real-time location using Google Maps integration. The app provides accurate navigation and displays café information, making it easy to discover new coffee spots in your area.

QR Code-Based Reward System

Each café cup features a unique QR code. Users can scan the code to rate the product and earn points with every purchase. This innovative system encourages engagement and allows customers to provide instant feedback while building rewards that can be redeemed for free drinks.

Admin System for Café Owners

The admin system allows café owners to add their cafés to the platform, manage locations on the map, update café information, and control application content. Owners can track customer engagement and monitor the redemption of rewards, creating a complete café management solution.
